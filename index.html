Start here — get your free key

Everything on this site is 100% free. No subscription, no credit card, no account needed here. You only need a free Google account.
  1. Go to Google AI Studio. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey in a new tab and sign in with any Google account.
  2. Click "Create API key." Pick or create a project when it asks — either option is fine, it doesn't matter which.
  3. Copy the key. It's a long string that starts with "AIza". That's your key.
  4. Important — make sure you're on the free model. This step is required. In AI Studio, look at the left sidebar and click "Rate limits." On the right side of that page, you'll see a model selector — change it to Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite. This is the free model this site uses. Other models may charge you or not work correctly here.
  5. Paste your key above in the "YOUR GEMINI API KEY" field at the top of this page, then click "Set key." You'll see a green ✓ confirmation when it's saved.
You do not need to do anything else in AI Studio. This site handles everything from here. Your key is stored only in your current browser session and cleared automatically when you close this tab.

Your usage limits

Because everyone brings their own key, there's no shared pool to run out of. Your limits are yours alone.

500
requests per day

Every click of "Generate," and every message in a case-prep chat, counts as one request. 500 is a lot, most sessions use well under 50.

15
requests per minute

The tighter limit in practice. If you click "Generate" many times in quick succession, you might briefly get an error. Wait a few seconds and try again.

These numbers come straight from Google's free tier for the model this site uses (Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite). Check your own limits any time at aistudio.google.com → Usage.
